Design Background — ACI 318-25 §18.10.6▾

ACI 318-25 §18.10.6.2 — Special boundary elements required when extreme fiber compressive stress exceeds 0.2f'c:

σmax = Pu/Ag + Mu·(lw/2)/Ig > 0.2f'c

Ag = lw·bw   Ig = bw·lw³/12   (gross section, uncracked)

Neutral axis depth (equilibrium with uniform web reinforcement):

β1 = max(0.85 − 0.05(f'c−28)/7, 0.65)c = (Pu + ρv·bw·lw·fy) / [bw·(0.85f'c·β1 + 2ρv·fy)]

Required boundary element length (§18.10.6.4):

lbe = max(c − 0.1lw, c/2) ≥ bw (SDC D/E/F)

Confinement — ACI §18.7.5.4 (applied via §18.10.6.4):

Ash/(s·bc) ≥ max[ 0.3(Ag,be/Ach−1)·f'c/fyt , 0.09·f'c/fyt ]

Ag,be=lbe·bw   Ach=(lbe−2cc)(bw−2cc)   bc=bw−2cc

so = min(100+(350−hx)/3, 150 mm)   smax = min(6db, so)

Design Background — EN 1998-1 §5.4.3.4 / §5.5.3.4▾

EN 1998-1 §5.4.3.4 / §5.5.3.4 — Boundary elements required when axial ratio exceeds the limit:

νd = NEd/(lw·bw·fcd) > 0.15 (DCH) or 0.20 (DCM)

Length of critical end region:

lc = max(0.15lw, 1.5bw) [DCH]lc = max(0.10lw, 1.5bw) [DCM]

Mechanical volumetric ratio (EN 1998-1 §5.5.3.4(4)):

ωwd ≥ max(30·μφ·νd·εsy,d·bc/bo − 0.035, 0.12)

εsy,d = fyk/(γs·Es)   Required confinement steel:

Ash/s = ωwd·Ach·fcd / (bc·fyd)

Design Background — ACI 318-25 §18.10.4▾

ACI 318-25 §18.10.4 — Shear strength of structural walls:

Vn = (αc·√f'c + ρt·fy)·Acv

αc = 0.25 when hw/lw ≤ 1.5; αc = 0.17 when hw/lw ≥ 2.0 (interpolate between)

Vn,max = 0.66·√f'c·Acv   φ = 0.75

Acv = lw·bw (net concrete area). All in MPa, mm units.

Design Background — ACI 318-25 PMM Fiber Model▾

The P-M interaction diagram is generated by sweeping the neutral axis depth c from 2lw (pure compression) to near zero (pure tension). At each c, equilibrium of the rectangular stress block with all fiber strips gives (Pn, Mn):

Pn = 0.85f'c·a·bw + ΣAs,i·fs,i − ΣAs,i·0.85f'c (in comp. block)φ = 0.65 to 0.90 based on net tensile strain εt

β1·c = a; εs,i = (c−xi)/c·0.003; fs,i = min(εs,i·Es, fy).

Design Background — EN 1992-1-1 §6.2.5▾

EN 1992-1-1 §6.2.5 — Design shear resistance at the interface:

VRdi = (c·fctd + μ·σn + ρ·fyd·(μ·sin α + cos α))·Ai

c and μ depend on surface roughness (Table 6.2). α = 90° for perpendicular reinforcement → sinα=1, cosα=0.

VRdi,max = 0.5·ν·fcd·Ai   ν=0.6(1−fck/250)
