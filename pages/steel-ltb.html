LTB Zones — AISC 360-22 §F2

Limiting Unbraced Lengths

Plastic limit Lp Lp = 1.76 · ry · √(E/Fy)
Inelastic limit Lr Lr = 1.95 · rts · (E / 0.7Fy) · √(X2 + √(X2² + 6.76(0.7Fy/E)²)) where X2 = J / (Sx · ho)
Effective radius of gyration rts rts² = √(Iy · Cw) / Sx

Cb Moment Gradient Factor

AISC 360-22 §F1 — Eq. F1-1

Cb for non-uniform moment diagrams Cb = 12.5Mmax / (2.5Mmax + 3MA + 4MB + 3MC) ≤ 3.0

where Mmax = absolute maximum moment in unbraced segment, MA = moment at 1/4 point, MB = moment at midspan, MC = moment at 3/4 point (all absolute values).

Limitations

Applicability

  • Compact sections only (flange and web satisfy compactness limits of AISC Table B4.1b).
  • Doubly-symmetric I-shaped members loaded in the plane of the web (AISC §F2).
  • Strong-axis bending only. Weak-axis bending is not subject to LTB (AISC §F6).
  • Loads applied at the shear center. If loads are applied at the top flange, Cb should be reduced per Commentary §F1.
  • Bracing must satisfy stiffness and strength requirements of AISC Appendix 6.
  • This tool does not check shear capacity (AISC §G2).
