Design Background▼
Web Splice Plates

Two identical plates are placed on opposite sides of the beam web (double-sided). The bolts pass through both plates and the web, putting each bolt in double shear: φRn,bolt = φFnvAb × 2 per bolt. Plate capacities (shear yield, shear rupture, block shear, bending) are computed for both plates combined (×2).

The eccentricity aw is the horizontal distance from the splice centerline to the bolt row centroid. It generates a secondary moment Vu·aw in the plates (plate bending §F11) and a horizontal force component at the critical bolt (elastic vector method).

Flange Splice Plates

One plate per flange (outside face), bolted in single shear. The flange force is computed as Pf = Mu / (d − tf), where d − tf is the moment arm between flange mid-planes.

Checks: bolt shear §J3.6, plate gross tension yield §J4.1 (φ = 0.90), plate net section rupture §J4.1 (φ = 0.75, one hole deducted in the critical cross-section width), bolt bearing on plate and beam flange §J3.10.
