1. Concrete — EN 206 & EN 1992

Concrete is specified by a strength class in the format C fck/fck,cube, where fck is the 28-day characteristic cylinder compressive strength and fck,cube is the equivalent cube strength. Cylinder strength governs EC2 calculations.

Key Concrete Design Values (EC2 §3.1)

EC2 — Design compressive strength
SI fcd = αcc · fck / γC MPa
Where:
αcc = 1.0 (EN recommended; many NAs use 0.85 or 0.8 for bending)
γC = 1.5 (persistent/transient); 1.2 (accidental)
Example C30/37: fcd = 1.0 × 30 / 1.5 = 20.0 MPa
EC2 — Secant elastic modulus
SI Ecm = 22 × (fcm/10)0.3 GPa
fcm = fck + 8 MPa  (mean cylinder strength)

Concrete vs US Notation

  • EC2 fck and ACI f'c are both 28-day cylinder strengths — numerically equal
  • C30/37 ≈ ACI 4,350 psi (30 MPa): a direct one-to-one comparison applies
  • ACI cube tests are not standard; UK-specified cube strengths (in the class suffix) exceed cylinder strength by ~25%

2. Reinforcing Steel — EN 10080

Reinforcement is classified by its characteristic yield strength fyk, ductility class (A, B, or C), and weldability. B500B (500 MPa, class B) is the most common grade in Europe for building structures.

EC2 — Design yield strength of rebar
SI fyd = fyk / γS MPa
γS = 1.15 (persistent/transient); 1.0 (accidental)
B500B: fyd = 500 / 1.15 = 434.8 MPa ≈ 435 MPa
Es = 200,000 MPa (fixed by EC2)
EC8 ductility requirement: For seismic design (DCM / DCH), minimum ductility class B (B500B or higher) is required for critical regions of ductile walls and frames. Class A is only permitted for DCL or non-critical members.

Rebar: Eurocode vs US

  • EU B500B fyk = 500 MPa vs US Grade 60 fy = 420 MPa — European bars are 19% stronger
  • Design value B500B: fyd = 435 MPa vs Grade 60 (ACI, no reduction): 420 MPa — approximately equal in practice
  • US ASTM A615/A706 bars; EU bars to EN 10080. Bar sizes differ: EU uses mm diameter (Ø8, Ø10, Ø12 … Ø32); US uses # designations (#3 = 9.5mm through #18 = 57mm)

4. Partial Safety Factors γM

Material partial factors reduce characteristic strengths to design values. EN 1990 Table A1.2(B) and the material Eurocodes specify recommended values; National Annexes may modify them.

UK National Annex note: UK NA reduces γM0 and γM1 to 1.00 (same as EN recommended) but requires αcc = 0.85 for concrete in bending, giving fcd = 0.85 × fck / 1.5 = 0.567 fck. Always confirm the applicable NA.

5. Exposure Classes & Durability

EN 206 defines exposure classes (XC, XD, XS, XF, XA) based on the environmental conditions causing concrete degradation. Each class requires a minimum concrete grade, maximum w/c ratio, and minimum cement content.

Cover Requirements

Minimum cover cmin,dur for corrosion protection (EC2 Table 4.4N, structural class S4, design life 50 years):

  • X0 / XC1: 10 mm (rebar) / 10 mm (prestress)
  • XC2 / XC3: 25 mm / 35 mm
  • XC4: 30 mm / 40 mm
  • XD1 / XS1: 35 mm / 45 mm
  • XD2 / XS2: 40 mm / 50 mm
  • XD3 / XS3: 45 mm / 55 mm

Nominal cover: cnom = cmin + Δcdev, where Δcdev = 10 mm (recommended).
