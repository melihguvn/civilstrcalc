1. Concrete — f'c and Elastic Modulus

A992 vs A572 — Key Differences

A992 adds requirements that A572 does not:

  • Yield-to-tensile ratio: Fy/Fu ≤ 0.85 (limits strain hardening — important for plastic design and seismic)
  • Carbon equivalent limit: Restricts weldability requirements for field connections
  • Chemistry limits: C ≤ 0.23%, Mn ≤ 1.60% — ensures consistent behavior

AISC 341-22 §A3.1 requires A992 (or equivalent) for wide-flange shapes in special moment frames (SMF) and intermediate moment frames (IMF).

5. Material Selection Guidelines

Concrete Grade

  • f'c = 3,000 psi (21 MPa): Footings, lightly loaded slabs-on-grade
  • f'c = 4,000 psi (28 MPa): Standard for beams, slabs, and columns in most buildings
  • f'c = 5,000–6,000 psi (35–41 MPa): High-rise columns (5+ stories), prestressed elements
  • f'c ≥ 8,000 psi (55 MPa): Special high-strength applications; requires special mix design and QC

Rebar Grade

  • Grade 60 A615: Default for non-seismic design; widely available and economical
  • Grade 60 A706: Required for seismic force-resisting elements (SMF, SSW) per ACI 318-25 Ch.18
  • Grade 80/100: Reduces reinforcement congestion in heavily loaded columns; verify code applicability

Structural Steel Grade

  • A992: Default for all W-shape wide-flanges; preferred even in non-seismic zones
  • A572 Gr.50: Angles, channels, plates; also W-shapes in non-seismic non-ductility applications
  • A36: Limited use today; primarily for small plates or where 50 ksi is unneeded
  • A500 Gr.C / A1085: HSS members; A1085 for seismic
Cost note: A992 and A572 Gr.50 are typically the same cost from steel service centers, making A992 the economical choice for wide flanges. Using higher-strength steel (Fy = 65+ ksi) requires careful connection and compact section checks — the higher yield strength does not automatically improve LTB or local buckling capacity.

6. Durability & Exposure Requirements — ACI 318-25 §19.3

ACI 318-25 §19.3.1 requires that concrete be designed for the most severe exposure class anticipated. Exposure classes are assigned separately for each of four exposure categories; the most demanding requirement for any assigned class governs the mix design.
