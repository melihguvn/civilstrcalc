1. Concrete Grades — TS 500 Table 2

TS 500:2000 Table 2 defines concrete classes by their characteristic cylinder compressive strength fck at 28 days (150 × 300 mm cylinder). The class notation Cck/Ccube follows the Eurocode convention where the first number is the cylinder strength and the second is the 150 mm cube strength (approximately fcube = 1.25 · fck).

TSC 2018 minimum concrete class: §5.4.1 requires C25/30 as the minimum concrete class for all structural elements (beams, columns, walls, slabs) in seismic design. C20/25 is only permitted for non-structural elements. In practice, C30/37 is standard for earthquake-resistant frames.

Tensile Strength fctk

TS 500 §4.2 gives the characteristic tensile strength as:

TS 500 §4.2 — Characteristic Tensile Strength
TS 500fctk = 0.35 · fck2/3MPa
Designfctd = fctk / γc = fctk / 1.5MPa

The tensile strength governs shear capacity (Vcr), punching shear, and splitting in development length calculations. It is not used directly for flexural design.

3. Reinforcement — TS 708:2010

TS 708:2010 classifies reinforcing steel by yield strength and ductility class. The letter suffix (A, B, C) indicates ductility — Class C has the highest ductility and is mandatory in seismic zones per TSC 2018.

TSC 2018 Seismic Requirements for Reinforcement (§5.4)

  • All longitudinal and transverse reinforcement in seismic systems must be Class C ductility (B420C or B500C).
  • The upper bound on fuk/fyk ≤ 1.35 is critical — excessively high actual strength would increase capacity shear demands unexpectedly.
  • B500C has become the standard specification in Turkish practice, replacing B420C for most new construction since the mid-2010s. The higher yield strength reduces steel quantities but requires careful crack width verification at serviceability.
  • Smooth bars (plain round) are not permitted for longitudinal reinforcement in structural elements per TSC 2018.
Actual vs characteristic strength: TSC 2018 §5.4.3 limits overstrength — the ratio fuk,actual/fyk,nominal must remain within 1.15–1.35. If mill certificates show excessive overstrength, capacity design forces (column shear, foundation loads) may be underestimated. Always check mill certificates for seismic projects.

6. TSC 2018 Material Requirements

TSC 2018 §5.4 imposes mandatory material quality requirements that supplement TS 500 for all elements in the seismic force-resisting system:

  • Minimum concrete class: C25/30 for all structural elements (fck ≥ 25 MPa). Higher grades (C30/37 or above) are recommended for columns in DTS 1/2 buildings.
  • Reinforcement ductility class: Class C mandatory — this means B420C or B500C only. B500C is the standard choice in modern practice.
  • Welded wire mesh: Only Class A or B welded fabric — not used for longitudinal bars in seismic members. May be used for secondary slab reinforcement with TSC acceptance.
  • Overstrength bound: For capacity design calculations, TSC §5.4.3 defines the overstrength factor D = fyk,actual/fyk,nominal. The design capacity forces amplified by D must be checked to ensure critical elements (columns, connections) remain elastic.

Material Properties for Capacity Design

When TSC 2018 requires capacity design (e.g., column design in §7.3, foundation design in §16), the expected material strengths replace design values:

TSC 2018 §5.4 — Expected Strengths for Capacity Design
Concretefce = 1.3 · fckexpected mean
Rebarfye = 1.2 · fykexpected mean

These expected strengths are used to compute probable flexural capacities (Mpr) of beams, which in turn determine the design shear of columns via the strong column–weak beam mechanism.

Practical Implications

  • Concrete Ec: TS 500 gives slightly lower stiffness than EN 1992, leading to slightly larger computed deflections — conservative for serviceability. ACI's formula yields the lowest modulus of the three.
  • Rebar strength: B500C (fyd=435 MPa) vs ACI Grade 60 (fy=420 MPa, with φ=0.90 → φfy=378 MPa) means TS 500/Eurocode designs use approximately 15% less steel for the same moment demand.
  • Concrete strain: EN 1992's εcu2=0.0035 vs TS 500's εcu=0.003 means neutral axis depth limits differ slightly. TS 500 is marginally more conservative in the maximum permissible moment capacity.
