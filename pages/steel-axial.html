Flexural Buckling Stress — AISC 360 §E3

The nominal compressive strength Pn = Fcr × Ag. Design strength φcPn with φc = 0.90.

Inelastic buckling — KL/r ≤ 4.71√(E/Fy)Fcr = 0.658Fy/Fe × Fy
Elastic buckling — KL/r > 4.71√(E/Fy)Fcr = 0.877 × Fe
Euler elastic buckling stressFe = π²E / (KL/r)²

Use the maximum of KL/rx and KL/ry. Recommended limit: KL/r ≤ 200.
